## 🌟 Core Features

### 📞 1. Ultra-HD Video & Audio Calling (WebRTC)
* **True 720p/1080p 30FPS Video**: Hardware-accelerated crystal clear video streaming.
* **48kHz Studio Opus Audio**: Stereo dual-channel voice with hardware Echo Cancellation, Noise Suppression, and Auto Gain Control.
* **WhatsApp-Style Floating In-App Mini Screen (PiP)**: Back button dabane par floating draggable video window active rehti hai with live stream, mic controls & edge-snapping physics.
* **Tap-to-Swap Screen Switching**: Single tap on floating preview swaps big screen and small screen seamlessly with tactile haptics.
* **Global STUN/TURN Relays**: Zero connection drops across 4G/5G, strict NATs, and Wi-Fi networks.

---

### 📺 2. Live TV & Entertainment Studio (PC & Mobile)
* **Desktop Cinema Split Layout**: 16:9 Cinema Stage on the left with live channel guide & instant zapper on the right for PC.
* **Over 200+ Live Channels**: News, Movies, Music, Sports, Regional & Global broadcasts.
* **6-Second Fast Watchdog & Smart VPN**: Auto-resolves geo-blocked or slow streams through Cloudflare Worker proxy tunnels.
* **Live EPG Schedule Card**: Real-time program progress indicator and upcoming show details.

---

### 🍿 3. Synchronized Watch Party & Live Voice Rooms
* **Zero-Lag Room Sync**: Watch movies, shows, and web links together in synchronized real-time.
* **Push-to-Talk & Open Mic**: Real-time low-latency multi-peer voice chat with animated speaking waves.
* **Live In-Room Chat & Reactions**: Floating emojis, timestamps, and interactive viewer controls.

---

### 💬 4. Encrypted Direct Chat & Media Hub
* **Clamped Swipe-to-Reply**: Drag resistance physics with instant quote preview.
* **Click-to-Scroll & Glow Highlight**: Tap any quoted message to smoothly scroll and highlight the original text for 2 seconds.
* **Real-Time Presence**: Online/Offline status, live typing indicators, and instant profile sync.
* **QR Quick Connect**: Scan to connect and start conversations instantly.

---

### 🎬 5. Powerhouse Video Player & Media Engine
* **Universal Codec Support**: MKV, 10-bit HEVC x265, MP4, HLS M3U8, and YouTube live feeds powered by `libmpv` (MediaKit).
* **Picture-in-Picture (PiP) & Background Play**: Background audio listening with lock-screen media controls.
* **Multi-Audio Track & Subtitle Switcher**: Switch language tracks and styles on the fly.
